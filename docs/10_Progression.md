# Progression Design — Gameplay & Crafting

**Owners:** Lead Gameplay Designer, Game Director
**Core thesis:** progression = knowledge (player skill + Journal) + infrastructure (tools, base, food/water security) + access (regions survivable). No XP, no levels, no character stats growth (skills postponed).

---

## 1. The intended player arc (Survivor difficulty)

| Act | In-game days | Player state | Region | Emotional beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I — Naked & Afraid** | 1–5 | bare hands → tier 1–2; first fire, first shelter, first safe water; first night terror | Coast | vulnerability |
| **II — Foothold** | 5–15 | camp with fire/smoker/rain collector; bow; hunting small game; first disease survived; river exploration | Coast → Lowland | competence dawning |
| **III — The Deep Forest** | 15–35 | workbench base (maybe treehouse); expeditions with prepared supplies; tracking large game; swamp raids for medicine; navigation mastery | Lowland, Swamp, Caves | mastery & awe |
| **IV — The Heights** | 35+ | tier-5 gear, cold-weather clothing, established supply chain; mountain expedition; summit | Highlands → Mountains | earned transcendence — looking down over everything survived |

The arc is **geographically enforced, never gated**: nothing stops a day-2 sprint at the mountains except honest simulation (cold, distance, no supplies). Speed-runners get authentic challenge; normal players get natural pacing.

### Milestone experiences (designed moments, not quests)
First self-made fire · first full-belly safe-shelter storm night (rain on your own roof) · first bow kill after a real stalk · surviving first systemic infection · first treehouse dawn · first raft river descent · navigating by stars for the first time · the summit.

## 2. Knowledge progression tracks (Journal-recorded)

- **Botany (~40 entries):** each plant unknowns → identified (observation/notes/risky tasting) → uses known. Misidentification is the early poison risk; mastery makes the swamp a pharmacy.
- **Zoology (~24 species):** behavior pages fill by observation (tracks read, kills witnessed, feeding seen) — filled pages literally make hunting easier (the player now *knows* peccaries drink at dawn).
- **Craft & construction:** recipe sketches (04 doc A1).
- **Cartography:** the self-sketching map; landmark bearings; star navigation (taught by a found note).
- **Medicine:** treatments annotated as experienced/learned.
- Journal completion is the collectible meta-game for completionists — cosmetic reward only (journal cover upgrades), never stat power.

## 3. Crafting progression tree (tier gates & intended pace)

```
T0 hands (min 0) ──> T1 primitive (min ~20: flake, cordage, spear, hand-drill fire)
   └─> T2 camp (day 2–4: mat+fire stations → bow drill, snares, bone tools, hide)
        └─> T3 homestead (day 5–10: workbench → bow, smoker, filter, canteen, adze, mud brick)
             └─> T4 established (day 12–25: grinding slab + first note finds →
                  polished tools, tailored clothing, rod & net, raft, advanced medicine)
                  └─> T5 mastery (day 25+: deep-exploration notes → salvage rework,
                       solar still, precision tools, cold-weather gear ── unlocks Act IV)
```

Rules: every tier's *gate item* (mat, workbench, slab) requires materials from progressively deeper regions — crafting pulls exploration, exploration feeds crafting. Each tier makes a previously tedious loop comfortable (T2 bow drill vs hand drill; T3 canteen vs leaf sips; T4 net vs spear fishing) — progression is felt as *time-and-risk economics*, not bigger numbers.

## 4. Building progression

Lean-to (hour 1) → lashed hut (day 2–4) → homestead compound w/ water & food infrastructure (day 5–15) → hardwood/mud-brick fortified base, treehouse, bridges (day 15–30) → stone-founded estate + outposts + raft network (endgame expression). Outposts (small forward camps in each region) become the natural late-game logistics pattern — and the architecture future settlements will inhabit.

## 5. Difficulty × progression tuning

Explorer compresses the arc (~60% pacing: faster insight prompts, gentler disease) — same tree, same order. Wilderness stretches it and removes insight *prompts* (recipes still learnable by pure experimentation and notes). One tree, three speeds — tuned via the same Data Asset policy as everything else.

## 6. Anti-frustration guarantees (the "rewarding not frustrating" contract)

- No unrecoverable death spirals: every condition has a treatment reachable from its onset state; worst-case collapse near shelter, wake weakened but alive (Survivor).
- No timers that punish absence-of-knowledge invisibly: everything that worsens, signals first (03 doc §2.2).
- Material re-farm pain capped: destruction refunds, tools repairable, dropped-gear retrieval on death (default).
- The Journal never withholds what the player has genuinely experienced — it is memory, not a slot machine.
